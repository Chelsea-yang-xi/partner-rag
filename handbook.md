## Distributor Program Overview

This handbook is a fictional demonstration dataset created for a software engineering portfolio project. It does not represent actual DJI commercial terms, internal policies, contracts, pricing rules, or partner requirements.

Authorized distributors purchase eligible products for resale through approved retail and e-commerce channels. Participation in the distributor program requires an active distributor agreement, verified business information, approved sales channels, and compliance with the policies described in this handbook.

Distributors remain independent businesses. Nothing in this handbook creates an employment, agency, joint venture, or franchise relationship.

## Distributor Onboarding

A new distributor must complete business verification before commercial orders can be processed. Required information includes the registered company name, registration number, tax information, billing contact, shipping contact, authorized purchasing contact, and approved sales channels.

The distributor account becomes active only after commercial approval is completed.

Account credentials must not be shared with unauthorized third parties.

Material changes to company ownership, billing information, shipping location, or authorized sales channels must be communicated to the partner account manager.

## Authorized Products

Distributors may sell only products included in their approved product scope.

Product categories may include camera drones, handheld imaging products, action cameras, gimbals, accessories, batteries, charging equipment, and selected robotic products.

Certain products may require additional certification, training, geographic approval, or channel approval.

A distributor must not modify serial numbers, product labels, safety markings, packaging identifiers, or regulatory information.

## Geographic Sales Territory

Products may only be actively marketed and distributed within the commercial territory defined in the distributor agreement.

Cross-border resale outside the approved territory requires written commercial approval.

Passive customer inquiries from outside the territory must be handled according to the distributor agreement and applicable law.

The distributor must not intentionally redirect inventory into unauthorized geographic markets.

## Recommended Retail Pricing

The supplier may communicate a Manufacturer Suggested Retail Price, also referred to as MSRP, for commercial planning purposes.

Unless local law or a separate agreement permits otherwise, the distributor remains responsible for independently determining its resale price.

Temporary promotional pricing may be communicated through approved campaign programs.

Unauthorized claims that a promotional price is officially sponsored by the supplier are prohibited.

## Purchase Orders

Commercial orders must be submitted through the approved ordering process.

Each purchase order must contain the distributor account identifier, product SKU, requested quantity, billing information, shipping destination, and purchasing contact.

An order is not considered accepted until order confirmation has been issued.

Requested quantities may be adjusted when inventory is constrained.

Duplicate purchase orders must be reported immediately to the account manager.

## Minimum Order Requirements

Certain SKUs may have minimum purchase quantities.

Minimum quantities can vary by product type, packaging format, logistics configuration, or promotional program.

If an order does not meet the applicable minimum quantity, the distributor may combine eligible products where permitted or request commercial review.

Special launch allocations may have separate quantity rules.

## Payment Terms

Payment terms are defined in the distributor agreement and commercial account setup.

Possible arrangements may include prepayment or approved credit terms.

Invoices must be paid using the bank details shown on the official invoice.

A distributor must verify unexpected requests to change bank details directly with its authorized commercial contact before transferring funds.

Overdue balances may result in order suspension or credit review.

## Rebate Policy

Eligible distributors receive a quarterly volume rebate of 2% when qualified quarterly sell-through exceeds EUR 200,000.

The 2% rebate is calculated on eligible net product value after approved discounts, returns, credits, and excluded promotional items.

Rebate eligibility requires valid sell-through reporting and an account in good commercial standing.

Rebate calculations are reviewed after the relevant quarter closes.

Disputed rebate calculations must be raised with the account manager together with supporting transaction records.

## Promotional Programs

Promotional programs may include temporary discounts, bundle support, launch incentives, retail visibility support, or approved sell-through incentives.

Participation requires confirmation from the account manager before the promotion begins.

Promotional support applies only to eligible SKUs, approved channels, and the specified promotion period.

Unused promotional support cannot automatically be transferred to another campaign.

Claims must contain the evidence required by the specific promotion.

## Inventory Management

Distributors are responsible for maintaining commercially reasonable inventory levels.

Inventory planning should consider expected sell-through, seasonality, product launches, promotion periods, and product transitions.

The supplier may provide non-binding forecasts or allocation guidance.

Distributors remain responsible for their own inventory purchasing decisions.

Products approaching end-of-life status may be subject to special purchasing or promotional arrangements.

## Product Launches

New products may be subject to launch confidentiality, allocation limits, embargo rules, and approved announcement timing.

A distributor must not publish confidential launch information before the authorized announcement.

Launch inventory may be allocated according to market opportunity, channel coverage, historical performance, and available supply.

Marketing assets provided before public announcement must be handled as confidential material.

## Delivery and Logistics

The distributor must inspect incoming shipments promptly after receipt.

Delivery problems, including missing cartons, visible transport damage, incorrect quantities, or incorrect products, must be reported within 72 hours after receipt.

A delivery problem report must contain the purchase order number, shipment reference, affected SKU, affected quantity, description of the problem, and supporting photographs when relevant.

Failure to provide sufficient evidence may delay investigation.

Carrier tracking information does not by itself confirm product condition.

## Returns and RMA

Products must not be returned without an approved Return Merchandise Authorization, also referred to as an RMA.

The distributor must provide the product serial number, purchase reference, reason for return, product condition, and requested resolution.

Approved returns must follow the packaging and shipping instructions provided with the RMA.

Products returned without authorization may be rejected.

Commercial returns and technical warranty returns are handled through separate processes.

## Warranty Policy

Eligible robotic vacuum products include a two-year limited warranty from the applicable consumer purchase date, subject to the fictional terms of this demonstration handbook.

Warranty service covers qualifying manufacturing defects under normal intended use.

Warranty coverage does not include deliberate damage, unauthorized modification, unsupported repair, misuse, or damage caused by operation outside published product requirements.

The distributor must direct warranty cases through the approved service process.

Proof of purchase and product serial information may be required.

## Technical Support

Distributors must maintain a reasonable first-line support capability for products they sell.

First-line support includes confirming the product model, collecting the serial number when relevant, checking basic setup information, and identifying whether the issue concerns operation, logistics, warranty, or commercial support.

Issues requiring engineering investigation must be escalated through the approved technical support channel.

A distributor must not promise unreleased software fixes or unconfirmed product features.

## Marketing Materials

Only approved brand assets may be used when representing products as officially branded products.

Product names, logos, photographs, specifications, and campaign materials must not be intentionally altered in a misleading manner.

Marketing claims must accurately reflect published product functionality.

A distributor must not advertise experimental, unannounced, or unsupported capabilities as commercially available features.

Local translations must preserve the meaning of approved product claims.

## Online Marketplace Policy

Sales through online marketplaces are permitted only where the marketplace or seller account is included within the distributor's approved commercial scope.

Marketplace listings must accurately identify the product, condition, included accessories, and seller.

Listings must not falsely imply that the distributor is the manufacturer.

Counterfeit products, unauthorized refurbished products, altered serial numbers, and misleading product bundles are prohibited.

The distributor is responsible for marketplace customer service associated with its seller account.

## Brand Protection

The distributor must use trademarks and brand identifiers in accordance with approved brand guidelines.

Domain names, social media accounts, marketplace names, or store names must not falsely imply ownership by the manufacturer.

Suspected counterfeit products or unauthorized use of brand assets may be reported to the partner account manager.

The distributor must cooperate reasonably with legitimate brand-protection investigations involving its own sales activity.

## Channel Conduct

Partners must conduct business professionally and must not use deceptive, abusive, discriminatory, or intentionally misleading sales practices.

Confidential commercial information received through the partner relationship must be protected from unauthorized disclosure.

Questions unrelated to distributor policy are outside the scope of this handbook. Questions such as what time employees should sleep are personal matters and are not answered by the partner support system.

The distributor must not misrepresent competitor products, fabricate customer reviews, create false product scarcity, or impersonate another seller.

Commercial disputes must be escalated through the appropriate business contact.

## Customer Data

Customer information collected by a distributor must be handled according to applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

Only information necessary for legitimate business, support, fulfillment, or warranty purposes may be collected.

Customer information must not be sold or disclosed to unauthorized parties through the distributor relationship.

Access to customer information must be limited to personnel who require it for legitimate business functions.

Security incidents involving relevant customer information must be escalated through the appropriate process.

## Product Safety

Products must be stored, transported, demonstrated, and sold in accordance with published safety information.

Damaged batteries, visibly deformed batteries, or products showing signs of serious physical damage must not be offered for normal resale.

Distributors must not remove required safety documentation from retail packaging.

Safety-related customer complaints must be escalated through the appropriate support process.

The distributor must not provide technical instructions that intentionally bypass published safety controls.

## Compliance

The distributor is responsible for complying with applicable laws and regulations relevant to its business activities.

This may include consumer protection, product safety, privacy, competition, advertising, tax, import, export, and trade requirements.

The distributor must not use the commercial relationship to facilitate unlawful transactions.

Requests involving restricted transactions or suspicious counterparties must be escalated through the appropriate compliance process.

## Sales Reporting

Where required by the distributor agreement, the distributor must provide accurate sell-in, sell-through, inventory, or channel reporting.

Reports must use the requested product identifiers and reporting format.

Knowingly submitting fabricated commercial data may result in commercial review.

Reported information may be used for forecasting, rebate calculation, inventory planning, channel analysis, and commercial support.

Material reporting errors must be corrected after discovery.

## Business Review

The account manager and distributor may conduct periodic business reviews.

A business review may cover sales performance, inventory position, product mix, upcoming launches, promotional activity, support issues, forecasts, channel opportunities, and commercial risks.

Forecasts discussed during a business review are planning estimates and do not automatically create purchase commitments.

Commercial decisions requiring formal approval must be documented through the applicable approval process.

## Escalation Process

Operational questions must first be directed to the distributor's normal partner contact.

Order and logistics issues must include the relevant purchase order or shipment reference.

Technical issues must include the product model, problem description, and relevant diagnostic information.

Commercial disputes must include supporting records and a clear description of the requested resolution.

Urgent safety or compliance concerns must be escalated through the designated priority process.

## Account Suspension

A distributor account may be reviewed or suspended for serious policy violations, suspected fraud, persistent payment default, unauthorized distribution activity, falsified reporting, misuse of confidential information, or significant compliance concerns.

Suspension may restrict ordering, promotional participation, rebate processing, or access to partner systems.

The distributor will be informed through the applicable commercial process when communication is legally and operationally permitted.

## Relationship Termination

Termination rights are governed by the distributor agreement.

Following termination, the distributor must stop representing itself as an authorized distributor once its authorization ends.

Confidential information, restricted partner materials, and access credentials must be handled according to contractual requirements.

Outstanding invoices, approved credits, open warranty cases, and authorized returns remain subject to the applicable commercial process.

## Handbook Scope

This handbook is designed only as fictional source material for a retrieval-based software demonstration.

If this handbook does not contain the requested information, the support system must respond with: "Not found in handbook."

The system must not invent a policy, price, entitlement, product feature, commercial promise, or contractual term that is absent from this handbook.
